The Idea

Google Maps tells you where you are.
TADRIJA tells you where you are in history.

Old Damascus is the oldest continuously inhabited city on earth. Every alley holds a story. Every door hides a courtyard. Every corner has been turned by two thousand years of people before you.

But a map won't tell you any of that. It will get you from one point to another and leave the soul of the place invisible.

TADRIJA is different. It walks with you — and as you move, the city speaks.
